              Critical Associations — Rockwood & Green 10e + AO Foundation

              Terrible Triad: Elbow dislocation + coronoid fracture + radial head fracture. All three injuries must be addressed: reduce dislocation, fix coronoid (suture anchor if small; ORIF if >50% height), fix/replace radial head, repair LCL, repair MCL only if unstable after lateral repair. Early motion in hinged brace.

              Essex-Lopresti: Radial head fracture + interosseous membrane disruption + DRUJ injury. Simple radial head excision is CONTRAINDICATED — causes proximal radial migration, DRUJ subluxation, ulnar impaction. Always examine DRUJ under anaesthesia for all radial head fractures.

              Monteggia equivalent (proximal radius): Radial neck fracture with associated ulna shaft fracture or dislocation — always obtain full-length forearm radiographs.
